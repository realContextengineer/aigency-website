AI-GENERATED PORTRAIT

30 years of experience bridging Applied Psychology, Computing, and Design.

AiGENCY Ltd is led by Karl, whose work brings together 30 years across applied psychology, computing and design, including 15 years in front-end web development. He holds an MA in Sound Design for the Moving Image from Bournemouth Media School. His experience in therapeutic environments informs a human-centred approach to AI: reducing cognitive load, respecting sensory sensitivity and making digital experiences more predictable.

AiGENCY combines strategy, interface design, web implementation, back-end integration and practical AI workflows. Trusted specialists can extend the team when needed, while the human direction and accountability remain with Karl.
