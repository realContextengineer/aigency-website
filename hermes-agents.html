HOW HERMES WORKS

From a real request to a reviewable outcome.

Hermes does not simply generate an answer. It works through a defined route, applying only the context, skills and permissions appropriate to that job.

  1. 01Real workA clear request, repeated task or operational problem.
  2. 02Approved contextRelevant instructions, records and knowledge only.
  3. 03Skills + toolsReusable capabilities and connected systems.
  4. 04Decision supportA draft, recommendation or proposed action.
  5. 05Human gateReview where judgement or commitment matters.
  6. 06Controlled handoffA visible result, action or next step.

INFERENCE Hermes uses model-based reasoning to support a decision. It does not replace accountable human judgement.
