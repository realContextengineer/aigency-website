Why Commodity AI Content Fails in Search

Search systems do not need another generic article. They need clear, useful information from people with real experience and a reason to be accountable for what they publish.

Does Google penalise content simply because AI helped write it?

Google’s public guidance focuses on quality and usefulness rather than whether a particular drafting tool was used. AI can help organise ideas, but it does not replace first-hand experience, fact checking, clear authorship or a genuinely useful answer.

What makes a local business article stronger?

  • Lived experience: explain what you have actually seen or done.
  • Specificity: include useful local context, real constraints and clear examples.
  • Accountability: show who wrote or reviewed the article and why they are qualified.
  • Original value: add an insight, process, result or perspective that a generic prompt would not produce.

Our approach

We use AI as an assistant for structure and editing, then keep the judgement, verification and local point of view human. That makes the content more useful to readers and more defensible for the business.
