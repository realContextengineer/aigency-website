CLEAR ANSWERS / BOURNEMOUTH · DORSET

Questions a business should be able to answer quickly.

Short, self-contained answers help people, search engines and future agents understand the service.

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is a disclosed voice agent that answers calls, understands routine requests and either completes an approved task or hands the caller to a person.

Can it book appointments?

It can check a connected calendar and prepare or make an appointment when the business has approved that action. This demo simulates the booking.

Can it transfer a caller to a real person?

Yes. Human handover should remain visible and easy to request. Ava sends a concise context summary before Sarah joins this demonstration.

Does it replace staff?

No. It can reduce repetitive call handling, while judgement, sensitive conversations and exceptions remain with the human team.

Could a Bournemouth small business use one?

A Bournemouth, Poole, Christchurch or wider Dorset business could use a voice agent for opening hours, service questions, appointment requests and structured callbacks.

Can it work with a CRM or calendar?

Yes, with the right permissions and integrations. The cards above show demo states only; no live CRM, calendar or phone data is connected here.
