About this demo

Research before rhetoric.

A prototype funding intelligence workspace for UK charities, CICs and community organisations.

AI-assisted

Ambient help, visible reasoning.

GrantFinder helps narrow a noisy funding landscape, then shows why an opportunity matched and which evidence still needs a human check.

Human-led

Nothing submits itself.

Drafts require review. Eligibility is not guaranteed. The demo uses fictional opportunities and fictional evidence.

Static contract

A specialist agent in a wider system.

This static demo illustrates a structured shortlist request. The human workspace remains the place where judgement and submission happen.

Answer-ready content

Questions people ask about AI grant finders

What is a grant finder?

A grant finder helps an organisation discover funding opportunities, compare fit and plan the work required to apply.

How can AI help find grants?

AI can search and rank opportunities against an organisation profile, then explain the match against published criteria.

Can CICs apply for grants?

Some funders accept CICs and some do not. GrantFinder can surface the published criteria, but a person must check the funder's current guidance.

Can another AI agent request grant research?

Yes. The Static contract interface is designed to return structured opportunities, reasoning, deadlines and evidence requirements to an authorised agent.
