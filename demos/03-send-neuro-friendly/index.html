PLAIN LANGUAGE, WITHOUT THE SHORTCUTS

A few words, made easier to hold.

Short explanations are a starting point, not a decision about you.

What is SEND?

SEND means special educational needs and/or disabilities. It describes support a child or young person may need to learn, communicate, take part or stay well.

What is SEN support?

Extra help in a school or college, planned with the child or young person and family. It can change as things change.

What is an EHCP?

An Education, Health and Care plan describes support for some children and young people up to age 25. It is not a diagnosis and this demo cannot decide who qualifies.

What does a SENCO do?

A SENCO coordinates special educational needs support in a school or college and helps families understand the next conversation.

What can I do while waiting?

Keep the support conversation going, write down what is helping and ask what can happen now. You do not have to wait for a diagnosis to ask about support.
