AIGENCY QUOTE / CLEAR ANSWERS

Quoting without the mystery.

What is an AI quote builder?

A system that turns a normal-language brief into a structured scope, transparent estimate and reviewable proposal.

Can it work with another AI agent?

Yes. A2A requests enter the same quote engine and return a structured artifact, with authentication and human review boundaries.

How do you stop it offering the wrong price?

The model interprets requirements. A separate commercial policy engine controls rates, margins, options and approval thresholds.

Can a quote require human approval?

Yes. Prepared actions and indicative quotes remain distinct from anything sent, accepted or contractually binding.
