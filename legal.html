Privacy & website information

A plain-English summary of how this site handles enquiries. This page is information, not a substitute for tailored legal advice.

Information you choose to send

If you contact AiGENCY, we may receive your name, email address, service interest and the contents of your message. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and discuss potential services.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information through this website. Please do not include confidential customer, health or financial information in an initial enquiry.

Forms and email

Contact-form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify Forms so that AiGENCY can receive and respond to your enquiry. You can also contact AiGENCY directly by email.

Cookies and analytics

The site may save a local cookie-consent preference in your browser. No analytics or cross-site profiling is configured here.

Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold about your enquiry, request correction, or ask us to delete it where applicable.
