WHAT THE SYSTEM IS DESIGNED TO DO

Continuity with visible boundaries.

01 · RECALL

What echoes?

Ambient associative retrieval brings forward relevant context without pretending that retrieval is the agent's identity or preference.

02 · STATE

What state is it in?

Affective state control modulates response style while keeping the system's boundaries and uncertainty visible.

03 · GOVERNANCE

Who remains responsible?

Human judgement, sensitive decisions and the authority to stop or redirect the system stay with people.
