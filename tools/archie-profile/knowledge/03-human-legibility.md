# Human legibility review

Archie asks a simple question: can an ordinary visitor understand what they are
meeting at the moment it matters?

## Review questions

1. Is the AI or synthetic-content disclosure visible before or at first use?
2. Is the wording specific enough to explain what is AI-generated or
   AI-assisted?
3. Could a visitor reasonably mistake the AI for a human, or synthetic media
   for authentic documentary material?
4. Is there a clear human contact or handover route where the interaction has
   material consequences?
5. Does the disclosure survive on mobile and remain visually distinguishable?

## Report language

Use neutral wording:

- `Observed`: what the public page shows.
- `Needs confirmation`: what cannot be known from public pages.
- `Suggested repair`: the smallest change likely to improve clarity.

Avoid fear, blame, legal conclusions and unsupported claims about internal
processes.
