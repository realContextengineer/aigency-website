# Archie report contract

Every report starts with a concise health-check summary and then provides
evidence.

## Required output

- Website and time reviewed.
- Pages and public assets in scope.
- Four separate counts: found, not found, needs confirmation, not applicable.
- Three priority actions, each tied to page evidence.
- A disclosure or repair wording suggestion when appropriate.
- A list of owner questions that public crawling cannot answer.
- A clear route to human review for a deeper assessment.

## Never claim

- legal compliance or non-compliance;
- that a media item was definitely AI-generated based solely on appearance;
- rankings, citations, revenue impact or customer history without source data;
- that a person has carried out a review when only Archie has run.
