WHAT THE SCAN CHECKS

Sixteen public-surface signals. One clear report.

The scan is deliberately evidence-led. It shows what was found, what was missing and what your organisation must confirm.

HOW TO READ THE RESULT

Useful before you open a single tab.

Found

Public evidence was visible. The report gives you the page and the snippet.

Gap

No useful public evidence was found. That is a prompt to review, not a legal verdict.

Confirm

Some questions cannot be proved by crawling. They are clearly marked for a person.
