A2A DESIGN · FAQ

Questions about agent systems and interface design.

Short answers about A2A integration, AI agents, digital products and AI discovery.

What are A2A services?

A2A services design and connect agent-to-agent systems so agents can discover one another, delegate defined tasks and exchange structured results.

What is MCP?

MCP provides a standard way for AI systems to connect to tools and information sources with defined access rules.

Can A2A Design build an AI agent?

Yes. A2A Design works on agent capabilities, tools, skills, workflows and interfaces, including Hermes systems.

What does interface design include?

Web, mobile and agent-facing interfaces, design systems, prototypes and AI-assisted implementation.

What is vibe coding?

Vibe coding is an iterative AI-assisted development workflow. A2A Design combines it with interface design, testing and project structure.

What does AI search work cover?

Technical SEO, crawlability, structured information and content that search and answer systems can interpret.
