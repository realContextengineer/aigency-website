ARTICLE 50 · FROM 2 AUGUST 2026

If your website uses AI, people may need to be told.

Article 50 of the EU AI Act introduces transparency duties for certain interactive AI systems and AI-generated content. If a visitor meets a chatbot or AI agent, they should not have to guess that it is artificial.
