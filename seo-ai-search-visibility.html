WHAT THE SCAN CHECKS

Twenty-two signals that a crawler can actually evidence.

The single-page check runs 15. The optional five-page crawl expands the picture. Anything that needs a human is labelled as such.

FoundGapConfirm
01 Crawler access & indexability 5 checks

Robots rules for key AI crawlers, sitemap availability, canonical consistency, indexability signals and HTTP/HTTPS redirects.

  • Robots evidence per crawler
  • Sitemap and canonical signals
  • Noindex and redirect checks
02 Machine-readable discovery 3 checks

Whether helpful public files exist and can be read without pretending that emerging conventions are formal standards.

  • llms.txt and link validity
  • llms-full.txt where present
  • Robots references and related public files
03 Schema & entities 4 checks

Visible structured data and the organisation signals a machine can connect to the public business.

  • JSON-LD syntax and types
  • Organisation, Website and Service coverage
  • Core properties and public identity links
04 Content evidence & answer shape 4 checks

How clearly a page answers its own subject, supports its claims and exposes extractable passages.

  • Heading hierarchy and direct answers
  • Evidence near substantive claims
  • Authorship, freshness and passage shape
05 Semantic HTML & technical health 3 checks

Semantic landmarks, useful alt text and the places where rendering can alter what a crawler receives.

  • Structural HTML signals
  • Image alt coverage
  • Render and performance flags
06 Internal architecture 3 checks

Only available with the five-page crawl: reachability, orphan risks and whether service information has a coherent structure.

  • Key-page click depth
  • Service/contact/about connectivity
  • Topic and link-pattern coherence

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Evidence, not a pretend prediction.

Every result is severity-ranked and tied to something observable: a robots rule, a schema block, an extracted answer, a heading pattern or a public link.

Read the thinking behind useful AI-search content ↗
01Observable signal

The exact public URL, code fragment or page pattern the scan found.

02Severity-ranked issue

Critical, warning or information—so the report does not flatten every finding into one score.

03Practical next step

A copy-paste fix where possible, or a clear flag for human review where it is not.

HONEST LIMITATIONS

What a public crawl cannot tell you.

  • Whether ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google will cite a page.
  • Off-site authority, brand mentions, PR or reputation.
  • Query-level inclusion, competitor position or commercial return.
  • Whether human expertise, credentials and experience are credible.

Those questions belong in the human-led audit, not in a score dressed up as certainty.
