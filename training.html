COMMUNITY AI CONFIDENCE · ONLINE & LOCAL

Learn to use AI safely, clearly and confidently.

Practical, human-supported training for people, charities and organisations who want to use AI without feeling overwhelmed.

A CALM, PRACTICAL SESSION

Learn it, practise it, check it.

Sessions start with a real question and leave room to practise, repeat and ask for help.

  1. 01QUESTIONStart with the real taskIdentify what you want to understand, check or make easier.

WHO THIS IS FOR

Support that starts where people are

Training is for people with different levels of digital experience and confidence, including individuals, neurodivergent adults, disabled people who need accessible digital support, older or low-confidence digital users, charity staff and volunteers, sole traders and small organisations.

People do not need to be technical. The pace and examples should fit the person and the task.

SAFE EVERYDAY USE

What people can learn

  • What AI is and where people encounter it
  • Privacy, personal information and passwords
  • Phishing, fraud, fake messages and misleading content
  • How to check an AI-generated answer
  • When a human decision is needed
  • When to stop and ask for help

ONLINE & LOCAL DELIVERY

Learn in the format that suits the work

Training can include online tutorials, live Zoom sessions, guided practice on a low-risk task, local workshops through charities and community organisations, and staff or volunteer training.

Where arranged for the programme, support can include plain-English written guidance, screenshots, captions, transcripts, pauses and repetition.

CHARITY & COMMUNITY PILOTS

A defined local project, not a vague promise

AIGENCY can work with charities and community organisations on a defined AI confidence and safety pilot. A pilot can include a needs discussion, practical group sessions, Zoom drop-ins, a staff workshop, participant feedback and a short outcomes report.

The examples, pace and format should be shaped around the people the programme is designed to support.

PROGRAMME STATUS

Under development, with defined pilot enquiries welcome

This programme is not currently advertised as a scheduled, accredited or funded course. Dates, places, duration, price and funding status are agreed only for a defined pilot or commissioned session. Use the contact route to discuss a suitable format.

ACCESSIBILITY & COMMUNICATION

Clear, predictable and flexible

Support should be clear, patient and flexible. The aim is to give people practical confidence, not to make them dependent on a tool or a consultant.

Any accessibility arrangement is agreed for the programme rather than assumed or promised without preparation.

CLEAR BOUNDARIES

What this training is not

This is non-clinical education and support. It does not provide therapy, diagnosis, crisis support, care assessments, or legal, medical or financial advice.

AI should not be treated as an authority simply because its answer sounds confident. Important information needs an appropriate source and, where needed, a human decision.

EVIDENCE BEHIND THE APPROACH

Grounded in digital inclusion, safe practice and human oversight

The programme is shaped by UK guidance on digital inclusion, AI skills, accessible learning, privacy, human oversight and online safety. Guidance and tools change; organisation-specific legal, safeguarding, data-protection or clinical questions may need qualified advice.

Digital Inclusion Action Plan · Skills for AI · NCSC phishing guidance · ICO AI and data protection guidance

TRAINING QUESTIONS

Clear answers before people enquire

Who is the training for?

It is for individuals, neurodivergent adults, disabled people needing accessible digital support, older or low-confidence digital users, charity staff and volunteers, sole traders and small organisations.

Do I need technical experience?

No. Sessions start with everyday tasks, use plain English and leave time to pause, repeat and ask questions.

Is it available on Zoom?

Yes. Online tutorials and live Zoom sessions are part of the proposed delivery options.

Can charities commission a pilot?

Yes. The scope, format, accessibility arrangements, funding status and price are agreed before any work begins.

What does the training cover?

It covers everyday AI use, privacy, scams and misleading content, checking AI answers, human decision-making and when to stop and ask for help.

Is this medical, therapeutic or legal advice?

No. This is non-clinical education and support, not therapy, diagnosis, crisis support, care assessment, or legal, medical or financial advice.
